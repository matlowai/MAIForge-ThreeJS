What you are seeing

Climate colours visualize temperature and precipitation. Rock colours and grain are world-anchored appearance priors. They are not a separate geological or ecosystem simulation. Native water levels are preserved.

Three.js handles rendering and browser input. MAIForge owns generation, rules and source evidence. Canonical gameplay physics and edits are not replaced by this inspection camera.
