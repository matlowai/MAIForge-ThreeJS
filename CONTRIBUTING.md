# Contributing to MAIForge

MAIForge is publicly licensed under AGPL-3.0-only. **MATLOWAI handles official
separate-license negotiations**, including commercial and proprietary licensing.

Before submitting code or other project material, read the
[MATLOWAI Contributor Agreement v1.0](docs/legal/CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT_v1.0.md).
You retain your copyright while granting MATLOWAI the additional permissions
needed to offer your contribution as part of MAIForge under separately negotiated
terms. This includes alternative commercial/proprietary licensing without further
consent or royalties unless separately agreed in writing.

## Pull requests

Explain the change, list the checks you ran, and complete the agreement
acknowledgement in the PR template before submission. Disclose third-party
material and obtain any employer or coauthor permissions that are needed. Bug
reports and discussion alone are not a contribution-license acceptance.

## Maintainer acceptance

Before merging, confirm that the contributor personally recorded agreement to the
stated version and has represented that they have the necessary authority. Keep
that record associated with the submitted revision. For multiple rights holders,
obtain each acknowledgement or establish authorized representation. An unchecked
or deleted checkbox is not a waiver of this requirement. Do not tick it for someone
else or imply that a template checks legal authority automatically.

This is a maintainer-reviewed acceptance policy. The template is not an automated
CLA bot or a configured required status check. Changes to agreement terms require
a new version; preserve previously accepted versions and their records.

For separate licensing, [say hello to **MATLOWAI**](https://github.com/matlowai/MAIForge/issues).
An alternative license may be free or negotiated to suit the project and its
circumstances. Individuals and small teams are welcome; you do not need a big
budget to start a conversation. Any license exception still needs a written
agreement.
